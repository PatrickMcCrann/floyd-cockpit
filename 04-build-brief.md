# Build Brief: Floyd Growth Decision Cockpit

*The one-pager handed to the AI coding agent. Fictional company, synthetic data only.*

## The company

**Copperline** (fictional): Series B B2B SaaS, workflow software. It is August 31, 2026. Eight months of actuals are on the books; September through December remain, plus the FY27 plan to set.

Where they stand: $12.0M ARR growing ~40% YoY, 479 customers across two tiers (Core ~$1,515/mo, Enterprise ~$6,550/mo), 70 heads, $449K monthly net burn, $8.7M cash, roughly 19 months of runway. $4.0M drawn on a venture term loan at 11%, with a covenant: cash never dips below $3.0M. Board plan says exit FY26 at $13.5M ARR and FY27 at $18M.

The decision on the table: the CEO wants to accelerate. Two paths. **Organic**: hire 4 AEs to power the Q4 bookings ramp. **Inorganic**: acquire Brightpath, a smaller competitor ($3.2M ARR, $7.5M ask, 85% gross retention, closes Nov 1). The CFO must tell leadership which path (or neither, or both) survives contact with cash, covenant, and capacity.

**The embedded conflict** (integrity check must catch it): the FY27 ramp requires $350K of net-new MRR in Q4, which needs 4 AEs *productive* by October (3-month ramp). The current plan starts them December 1. The target and the hiring plan contradict each other. Second flag: $42K of enterprise MRR is at risk at November renewal, and the base forecast assumes it renews.

## What to build

Streamlit app, Python/Pandas model, all math deterministic. LLM (API call) writes narrative only, from computed metrics passed as structured JSON. Bundled CSVs in `/data` (generated by `generate_data.py`); no uploader, no integrations.

**Screen 1 — Where we stand.** Actuals through August: ARR trajectory, burn, cash, runway, gap to the $13.5M FY26 target. One status line: on plan or off, and by how much.

**Screen 2 — Decision studio.** The levers: new logos/month, monthly churn, price increase at renewal (Oct 1), AE hires and start date, and an acquire-Brightpath toggle (price, cash vs. debt funding). Recompute on change: monthly forecast Sept–Dec, quarterly FY27, cash-goes-negative date if any, covenant headroom, runway, revenue bridge from $12.0M to the target.

**Screen 3 — The memo.** AI-written CFO decision memo: on/off track, the two to four drivers that matter, scenario and risk assessment, a specific recommendation, leading indicators with trigger thresholds. Regenerates per scenario.

**Integrity check** (deterministic rules, surfaced on screens 2 and 3): bookings capacity vs. target (AE count × ramp × quota vs. required net-new MRR), at-risk renewals assumed renewed, covenant breach in any scenario month, hiring ahead of capacity evidence, acquisition cash draw vs. minimum-cash guardrail. Output style: "Plan integrity: at risk," with the specific contradiction named and the two or three moves that would close it.

## Model rules

Revenue: customers × ARPU by tier; churn and new logos monthly; NRR applied to expansion; price increase hits at renewal only. Costs: payroll from headcount plan (loaded cost per AE $16.5K/mo; merit +4% Jan 2027); other opex carried at trend. Acquisition: adds Brightpath MRR at 85% retention, adds its $180K/mo opex minus 3 redundant roles from month 3, $400K integration over 6 months, purchase price out of cash (or $500K from the undrawn line), cross-sell +$25K MRR/mo from month 7. Cash: prior cash minus net burn, minus one-time items. Simplification is fine; consistency is required. Label the simplifications in the README.

## Acceptance criteria (definition of done)

1. Clones and runs end-to-end with one command; reviewer needs no setup beyond `pip install -r requirements.txt` and an API key.
2. Reviewer moves one lever and revenue, cash, runway, and the memo all update.
3. The memo names a specific decision with numbers, never a summary.
4. The integrity check fires on the bundled data (AE timing conflict and at-risk renewals) without any lever being touched.

## Demo storyline (3-minute Loom)

Open on the question every reviewer has been asked: "The board wants 49% growth next year. Can we afford the plan that gets us there?" Screen 1: on pace for FY26, but the FY27 ramp is not funded by the current plan, and the integrity check says so unprompted. Screen 2: try the organic path (pull AE starts to October); show burn, covenant headroom, runway. Flip on the acquisition; show the cash draw against the covenant floor and where the combined path breaks. Screen 3: the memo recommends a path with triggers. Close: "The math was always possible. What was missing was the tool that puts the CFO in the room before the decision instead of after it."
